## 📌 Project Overview

**Advertising Performance Analytics** is a Business Intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The primary objective is to explore how advertising investments across different media channels relate to overall sales performance.

The dashboard focuses on three major advertising channels:

- **TV Advertising**
- **Radio Advertising**
- **Newspaper Advertising**

By combining key performance indicators (KPIs), correlation analysis, and interactive visualizations, this project provides a clear overview of advertising performance and supports data-driven marketing decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the relationship between advertising expenditure and sales.
- Evaluate the performance of different advertising channels.
- Measure the correlation between marketing investments and sales.
- Compare advertising channels based on their relationship with sales performance.
- Transform raw data into meaningful business insights.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard includes several analytical components:

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Dedicated KPI cards provide an overview of advertising investments and correlation metrics, including:

- Total TV Advertising
- Total Radio Advertising
- Total Newspaper Advertising
- TV Correlation
- Radio Correlation
- Newspaper Correlation

### 2. Advertising vs. Sales Analysis

Scatter charts are used to investigate the relationship between advertising expenditure and sales for each channel.

- TV Advertising vs. Sales
- Radio Advertising vs. Sales
- Newspaper Advertising vs. Sales

These visualizations help identify potential patterns and relationships between marketing investments and sales outcomes.

### 3. Advertising Channel Correlation

A clustered bar chart compares the correlation values of different advertising channels, providing a comparative perspective on their relationship with sales.

## 🔍 Key Analytical Insights

This project provides a foundation for exploring several important business questions:

- Which advertising channel has the strongest correlation with sales?
- How does advertising expenditure relate to sales performance?
- Are there noticeable differences between advertising channels?
- Which channels may deserve further investigation when planning marketing budgets?

**Note:** Correlation indicates statistical association, not necessarily causation. Actual business decisions should consider additional factors and supporting analysis.
